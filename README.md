## Overview

### The Challenge

Build a responsive multi-step subscription form where users can:

- Complete all four steps of the form.
- Navigate backwards and update their selections.
- Select subscription plans and optional add-ons.
- Switch between monthly and yearly billing.
- Review their selections and confirm their order.
- Receive validation messages for invalid or missing inputs.
- View a responsive layout across different screen sizes.
